Step-by-Step Well Control Procedure

Key Physics & Rules of Thumb

1. Pressure Takes TVD, Strokes Take MD
Hydrostatic head depends only on True Vertical Depth. Measured depth is used only to compute fluid displacement volumes and stroke counts.
2. Follow the Drill Pipe Gauge
The drill string contains clean mud of known density. Annulus pressure fluctuates heavily as gas expands. Keep drill pipe pressure on schedule.
3. Respect Choke Lag Time
Pressure pulses travel at ~3,000–4,000 ft/s (approx. 2 seconds per 1,000 ft of depth). Make small, measured choke adjustments and wait for needle response.
4. Wait-and-Weight vs. Driller's Method
Wait-and-Weight weights the pits before circulation, keeping casing shoe pressure lower. Driller's circulates the influx out first with original mud, then pumps kill mud on a second circulation.
